## Executive Summary

Our assessment indicates that both district heating and heat pumps are viable options for this area. While both solutions are technically sound and meet relevant standards, heat pumps offer a slightly more favorable long-term cost of 120.1 €/MWh compared to district heating's 122.1 €/MWh. Furthermore, heat pumps present a lower carbon footprint, emitting 137 kg CO₂/MWh, which is a key factor in our decision. Although the cost difference is small, the environmental benefit and overall feasibility make heat pumps the preferred choice. It is important to note that our analysis shows this decision is sensitive to future uncertainties, with heat pumps being the preferred option in 57% of scenarios.
